What the model sees

Log-mel spectrogram — frequency (low → high, bottom → top) over time. This is the actual input the CNN classifies, not the raw waveform.

Trained on the GTZAN dataset (1,000 30-second clips, 10 genres) with a compact CNN. Test accuracy is roughly 30–34% — well above the 10% random baseline, but these are best guesses, not verdicts.
